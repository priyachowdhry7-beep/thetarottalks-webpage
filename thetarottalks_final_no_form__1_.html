Frequently Asked Questions

What information do I need to provide?

You'll generally need to provide your name and date of birth (day, month and year). If a selected analysis requires any additional detail, this will be explained before the report is prepared.

How accurate is numerology?

Numerology is a spiritual and symbolic system, and its interpretations are subjective rather than scientifically established predictions. Use the report as a tool for reflection and exploration, not as a substitute for professional advice.

Can numerology predict my future?

The report may discuss traditional Personal Year themes and cycles. It cannot guarantee specific events or outcomes; your choices and circumstances remain important.

What's the difference between the Mini and Full Report?

The Mini Report (₹499) includes core numbers, Life Path and Destiny, Name Spelling Analysis, Mobile Number Analysis, career themes and basic practices. The Full Report (₹799) includes more detailed Name Spelling and Mobile Number Analysis, plus relationship compatibility, missing-number themes and an extended forecast section.

When will I receive my report?

The target delivery time is within 24 hours after payment and receipt of all required details. If a delay is expected, you should be informed.

How is my personal information handled?

Information you submit is used to prepare your report, communicate about your order and deliver the finished file. Please share only the details needed to prepare your report and do not submit sensitive information that is not requested. For privacy requests, contact the business email listed in the Contact section. Order information is retained only as needed to prepare and deliver the report, handle support requests, and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request correction or deletion where legally permitted.
